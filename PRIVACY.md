# Privacy Policy for PricePick

**Last Updated: April 2026**

PricePick is designed to be a lightweight, secure, and privacy-first tool. Your trust is important to us, which is why we have built this application to function without collecting any of your personal data.

### 1. Data Collection
PricePick **does not collect, store, or share** any personal information. We do not have access to your contacts, location, photos, or any other private data on your device.

### 2. Local Storage
All your app preferences, including:
*   Selected Currency
*   Theme Mode (Light/Dark)
*   Percentage Toggle settings

are stored **locally on your device** using industry-standard local storage (AsyncStorage). This data stays on your phone and is never transmitted to our servers or any third parties.

### 3. No Third-Party Tracking
We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising IDs, or tracking pixels. Your usage of the app remains completely anonymous.

### 4. Internet Connectivity
PricePick does not require an internet connection to perform its core calculations, ensuring that your data never leaves your device.

---
*For any questions regarding this policy, please contact the developer via the official app distribution channel.*
