# Privacy Policy for PricePick

**Last Updated: April 28, 2026**

PricePick is a price comparison calculator. This policy explains what data is collected, by whom, and how it is used.

---

## What PricePick itself collects

PricePick does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on its own servers. The app has no backend.

The following preferences are saved **locally on your device only** using AsyncStorage and are never sent anywhere:

- Selected currency
- Theme (Light / Dark / System)
- Percentage display toggle

---

## Third-party services

To keep PricePick free and to support in-app purchases, we use two third-party services. Each operates under its own privacy policy.

### Google AdMob (advertising)

PricePick displays banner ads served by Google AdMob. To serve relevant ads, Google may collect:

- Advertising ID (resettable in your device settings)
- IP address and general location
- Device model, OS version, and language
- App interaction data

This data is used by Google to show ads and measure their performance. PricePick does not receive or store any of this data.

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses data from apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

To opt out of personalized ads, go to your device settings and reset or limit your Advertising ID.

### RevenueCat (in-app purchases)

If you purchase the "Remove Ads" option, the transaction is processed through Google Play. RevenueCat is used to validate and manage that purchase. RevenueCat may collect:

- A hashed device identifier
- Purchase receipt data (used only for validation)

RevenueCat does not receive your payment details. Those are handled entirely by Google Play.

- [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)

---

## Internet connectivity

All price calculations are performed locally on your device. An internet connection is used only to load advertisements.

---

## Children's privacy

PricePick is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

---

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under GDPR (EU/EEA), CCPA (California), or similar laws — including the right to access, correct, or delete data held about you by our third-party partners. Please refer to the Google and RevenueCat privacy policies linked above to exercise those rights.
